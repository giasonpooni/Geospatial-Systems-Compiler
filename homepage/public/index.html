About

Notation Systems · Industrial information

We turn heterogeneous industrial observation into verified state you can inspect, price, and move.

We operate provenance-bearing computational corpora for the physical economy. Our objects are organizations, sites and parcels, shipments and contracts. We keep lineage from source to served state.

Three domains

PAYLOAD
Goods, sites and voyages.
TRADEWIND
Commodity and contract state.
LANDSHARK
Parcels, zoning, design intent and the built site.

Four crafts

Modelling
Declare the object and preserve its identity.
Simulation
Explore time and consequence on a declared model.
Compilation
Bring observations into spatial, temporal and relational state.
Verification
Check the result, retain its limits and refuse a bad merge.

The globe is a read-only exhibition of synthetic state, not a live feed. The compiler and evidence stores are not running on this page. Its generalized geographic contours are rendered on an undistorted sphere; it is not a survey or satellite photograph.
